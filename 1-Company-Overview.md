* Vision document

* Functional requirements specification (FRS)
* Non-functional requirements (NFR)
* User stories and acceptance criteria
* Domain model
* Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

* Infrastructure diagrams
* Threat model (STRIDE)
* Risk register
* Cost estimation and FinOps report
* High-level and low-level design (HLD/LLD)
* CI/CD pipeline design
* Monitoring and observability plan
* Security and compliance checklist
* Disaster recovery and business continuity plan
* Executive architecture presentation

## Final Capstone

Students act as architects for **NovaMart Global**, now a Fortune 500–scale enterprise operating across multiple regions with AI-powered retail, logistics, payments, and analytics. They design an end-to-end enterprise solution covering infrastructure, networking, security, identity, data, AI, DevOps, governance, compliance, cost optimization, and operations, then present and defend their architecture as they would in a real architecture review board.
